右半屏显示链路对齐原厂，内核更新到 7.2.14，支持带触控板的键盘。

## 更新

- **右半屏显示**：右半屏由第二路显示链路驱动，它使用第一路的时钟。现在按原厂方式初始化这条链路：时钟起来后重新同步第二路的接收缓冲并切回正常的时钟选择，把第一路的分频同步到第二路，按面板配置设置发送强度和时序，同步完成后复位两路控制器；两路共用的显示控制器每帧只提交一次。这些设置与原厂一致。部分平板全新安装后出现的右半屏花屏与这条链路有关。
- **内核**：更新到 Linux 7.2.14，包含上游 619 个稳定版修复，其中有存储多队列与 Wi-Fi 上电时序的修复。
- **键盘**：带触控板的小米键盘会自动识别触控板，无触控板的键盘不受影响。

## 已知限制

- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

手写笔、指纹和休眠到磁盘仍不支持。保留默认扬声器音量上限。

Aligns the right-half display link with the stock software, updates the kernel to 7.2.14, and supports the keyboard with a touchpad.

## Changes

- **Right half of the display**: the right half is driven by the second display link, which runs from the first link's clock. This link is now brought up as the stock software does: after the clocks start, the second link's receive buffer is resynchronized and its clock selection is switched back to normal; the first link's dividers are copied to the second; the drive strength and timings follow the panel configuration; both controllers are reset once synchronization completes; and the display controller shared by both links is committed once per frame. The right-half corruption seen on some tablets after a fresh install is related to this link.
- **Kernel**: updated to Linux 7.2.14 with 619 upstream stable fixes, including fixes for storage multi-queue and Wi-Fi power-up sequencing.
- **Keyboard**: the Xiaomi keyboard with a touchpad now has its touchpad detected automatically; keyboards without a touchpad are unaffected.

## Known limitations

- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint and disk hibernation remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap.
