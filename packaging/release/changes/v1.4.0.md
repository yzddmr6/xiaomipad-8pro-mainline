主题：手写笔、相机与红外控制

新增普通笔、相机手动控制和红外发送支持。

## 更新

- **应用中心** 修复预装应用中心的中文显示；首次开机无需先联网更新即可正确显示中文。

- **充电顶栏** 连接有线电源时，GNOME 顶栏显示电池侧净充电功率和当前充电通路。

- **修复** 修复了极少数开机卡在启动画面的问题。修复了应用提交过大的 GPU 任务时可能触发内核警告或占用过多内存的问题。

- **Wi-Fi** 冷启动时先校准 WCN7860，再应用 22 dBm 的板级发射功率上限。实际功率受固件和监管域约束。

- **手写笔** 小米灵感触控笔 M80p 支持悬停、0–8191 压感和两个侧键。系统按蓝牙 HID 型号选择笔工具和屏幕校准；既有安装需从对应屏型的原厂配置补提普通笔校准。
- **相机控制** 使用 `piano-camera-ctl` 可在前摄或后摄预览期间切换自动与手动曝光、白平衡，设置曝光时间、模拟增益、数字增益及红蓝增益，并读取驱动接受的值。
- **后摄对焦** 后摄支持自动对焦，也可在手动模式下调整镜头位置。
- **隐私灯** 前后摄共用绿色隐私指示灯。任一摄像头工作时保持点亮，最后一路关闭后熄灭。
- **拍照补光** 后摄拍照补光使用限时闪光脉冲。相机按传感器帧序号匹配曝光起止时刻，只在整帧曝光落入闪光窗口时保存照片。
- **红外** SE15 红外发射器接入 Linux LIRC。使用 `ir-ctl` 可选择载波并发送脉冲序列。

- **Android 应用** Waydroid 图形包支持 Adreno 830 硬件渲染，可从 GNOME 启动 Android 应用。
- **充电与挂起** 接着有线充电器时，锁屏或合盖后屏幕关闭，系统保持唤醒，快充可持续至充满。充满后仍保持唤醒，拔线后恢复正常休眠。 接电脑 SDP/CDP 口时可正常空闲挂起；开发者模式会保持连接。
- **插线唤醒与恢复** 支持挂起时插入充电线唤醒平板。更新 USB 控制器和 Wi-Fi 的恢复处理，并修正 Adreno 故障中断的 GMU 保活寄存器。
- **快充与键盘** 支持小米 MiPPS、第三方 PPS、QC3 class B 和 QC3.5 电荷泵快充，以及固定 PD 9 V 充电。系统根据电源能力、电芯温度和电压调节电流，接近充满时逐步降流，由 buck 完成恒压收尾。可恢复的供电或温度异常解除后会重新尝试快充。 原装充电器实测从 45% 充至满电约 38 分钟。 键盘重连后恢复背光。

## 升级须知

从 v1.3.1 升级，解压软件包和回退附件，整组升级时使用 `piano-1.4.0` 和 `--previous piano-1.3.1-rollback-1.4.0`。完成后重启。

Theme: Pen, camera and infrared controls

Adds Focus Pen, manual camera controls and infrared transmission.

## Changes

- **App Center** The preinstalled App Center displays Chinese correctly on first boot without requiring an online update.

- **Charging indicator** While wired power is connected, the GNOME top bar shows net battery charging power and the active charging protocol.

- **Fix** Fixed a rare issue that could leave the tablet stuck on the startup screen. Fixed excessive memory use or kernel warnings when an application submitted an oversized GPU job.

- **Wi-Fi** The driver calibrates the WCN7860 at cold boot before applying the 22 dBm board transmit-power limit. Actual power is subject to firmware and regulatory limits.

- **Pen** The Xiaomi Focus Pen M80p supports hover, pressure from 0 to 8191 and two barrel buttons. The Bluetooth HID identity selects the tool and panel calibration. Existing installations need the M80p profile extracted from the factory configuration for their panel.
- **Camera controls** While either camera is streaming, `piano-camera-ctl` can switch exposure and white balance between automatic and manual modes, set exposure time, analogue and digital gain or red and blue balance, and read back the values accepted by the driver.
- **Rear focus** The rear camera supports autofocus and manual lens positioning.
- **Camera indicator** Both cameras share the green privacy indicator. It stays active until the last camera stream stops.
- **Flash photography** Rear flash photos use a timed pulse. The camera checks the physical frame sequence and saves a photo when the complete exposure falls within the flash interval.
- **Infrared** The SE15 infrared transmitter uses Linux LIRC. Use `ir-ctl` to select a carrier and send pulse sequences.

- **Android apps** Waydroid runs Android apps from GNOME with Adreno 830 hardware rendering.
- **Charging and suspend** With a wired charger connected, locking the tablet or closing the cover turns off the screen. The system stays awake so fast charging can continue until full. It stays awake while the charger remains connected and resumes normal sleep behavior after unplugging. The tablet can suspend when idle on an SDP or CDP computer port; USB developer mode keeps the connection awake.
- **Cable wake and recovery** Connecting the charging cable can wake the suspended tablet. Update USB and Wi-Fi recovery and correct the GMU keepalive register used by Adreno fault interrupts.
- **Charging and keyboard** Supports Xiaomi MiPPS, third-party PPS, QC3 class B and QC3.5 charge-pump fast charging, and fixed 9 V USB PD. Charging current follows the supply capability, cell temperature and voltage. Current tapers near full charge and the buck converter finishes the constant-voltage phase. Fast charging resumes after recoverable supply or temperature conditions clear. With the original charger, charging from 45% to full took about 38 minutes in a measured run. Keyboard backlight is restored after reconnection.

## Upgrade notes

To upgrade from v1.3.1, extract the package and rollback repositories. Select `piano-1.4.0` with `--previous piano-1.3.1-rollback-1.4.0` for the whole-release upgrade, then reboot.

## Known limitations

M80p requires factory calibration for the panel. Use the Waydroid system and vendor images listed in the installation guide.
