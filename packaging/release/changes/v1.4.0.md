主题：手写笔、相机与红外控制

新增普通笔、相机手动控制和红外发送支持。

## 更新

- 小米灵感触控笔 M80p 支持悬停、0–8191 压感和两个侧键。系统按蓝牙 HID 型号选择笔工具和屏幕校准；既有安装需从对应屏型的原厂配置补提普通笔校准。
- 使用 `piano-camera-ctl` 可在前摄或后摄预览期间切换自动与手动曝光、白平衡，设置曝光时间、模拟增益、数字增益及红蓝增益，并读取驱动接受的值。
- 后摄支持自动对焦，也可在手动模式下调整镜头位置。
- 前后摄共用绿色隐私指示灯。任一摄像头工作时保持点亮，最后一路关闭后熄灭。
- 后摄拍照补光使用限时闪光脉冲。相机按传感器帧序号匹配曝光起止时刻，只在整帧曝光落入闪光窗口时保存照片。
- SE15 红外发射器接入 Linux LIRC。使用 `ir-ctl` 可选择载波并发送脉冲序列。

## 已知限制

- M80p 的本机验证覆盖合成笔事件。实笔书写手感尚无本地样本。
- 红外验证未配备接收端，家电响应和有效距离没有实测数据。

Theme: Pen, camera and infrared controls

Adds Focus Pen, manual camera controls and infrared transmission.

## Changes

- The Xiaomi Focus Pen M80p supports hover, pressure from 0 to 8191 and two barrel buttons. The Bluetooth HID identity selects the tool and panel calibration. Existing installations need the M80p profile extracted from the factory configuration for their panel.
- While either camera is streaming, `piano-camera-ctl` can switch exposure and white balance between automatic and manual modes, set exposure time, analogue and digital gain or red and blue balance, and read back the values accepted by the driver.
- The rear camera supports autofocus and manual lens positioning.
- Both cameras share the green privacy indicator. It stays active until the last camera stream stops.
- Rear flash photos use a timed pulse. The camera checks the physical frame sequence and saves a photo when the complete exposure falls within the flash interval.
- The SE15 infrared transmitter uses Linux LIRC. Use `ir-ctl` to select a carrier and send pulse sequences.

## Known limitations

- Local M80p checks used synthetic pen events. Writing feel has not been assessed with a physical M80p.
- Infrared checks had no receiver. Appliance response and effective range have not been measured.
