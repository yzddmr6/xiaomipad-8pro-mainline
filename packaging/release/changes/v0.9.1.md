## 更新

- **右半屏显示**：右半屏由第二路显示链路驱动，它使用第一路的时钟。原来链路时钟在每帧的消隐期间要停下再启动约 174 次，现在亮屏期间一直运行，画面数据的传输方式不变。部分平板全新安装后出现的右半屏花屏与这条链路有关。

## 已知限制

- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

手写笔、指纹和休眠到磁盘仍不支持。保留默认扬声器音量上限。

Keeps the display link clock running while the screen is on.

## Changes

- **Right half of the display**: the right half is driven by the second display link, which runs from the first link's clock. The link clock used to stop and restart about 174 times per frame during blanking; it now keeps running while the screen is on, and the way picture data is sent is unchanged. The right-half corruption seen on some tablets after a fresh install is related to this link.

## Known limitations

- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint and disk hibernation remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap.
