修正右半屏显示链路初始化，改善花屏并完善显示驱动的资源管理。

## 更新

- **右半屏显示**：补齐第二路显示链路的初始化配置，改善部分 BOE 屏幕的右半屏横纹、撕裂和花屏。
- **显示稳定性**：修正驱动初始化时的重复资源释放，完善电源和时钟操作失败时的处理与资源回收。
- **作者信息**：设置 → 系统 → 关于新增作者及项目主页入口。

## 已知限制

- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

手写笔、指纹和休眠到磁盘仍不支持。保留默认扬声器音量上限。

Corrects initialization of the right-hand display link to reduce corruption and improve display resource management.

## Changes

- **Right half of the display**: completes initialization of the second display link to reduce horizontal lines, tearing and corruption on affected BOE panels.
- **Display stability**: fixes a duplicate resource release during driver initialization and improves error handling and cleanup when power or clock operations fail.
- **Author information**: Settings → System → About now includes the author and a link to the project homepage.

## Known limitations

- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint and disk hibernation remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap.
