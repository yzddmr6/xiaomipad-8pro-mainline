## 更新

- **相机曝光**：自动曝光不再在亮处附近来回闪动；遮挡或照亮镜头后约 1–3 秒回到正常亮度。
- **后摄亮度**：后摄增益调节更细，亮度变化更平稳。
- **相机信息**：前后摄像头向应用报告正确的画面尺寸和帧率信息，不完整的画面帧不再送给应用。
- **视频**：硬件解码在视频分辨率切换后正确结束旧的画面序列；硬件编码支持按需生成关键帧，推流和录屏时能更快恢复画面。
- **外接显示器**：修正 USB-C 显示器链路启动失败时的错误处理和资源释放。
- **电池**：常温下充电可充满到 100%；电池温度达到 48 °C 时自动降低充电截止电压。
- **应用中心**：修复应用中心等使用 Flutter 新渲染器的应用不显示文字的问题。
- **Wi-Fi**：修正路由器调整信道或带宽后，无线统计信息反复等待超时的问题。

## 已知限制

- 插过 USB-C 显示器后，再用 USB 线连接电脑使用开发者连接，需要重启平板才能识别。
- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

Steadier camera exposure, more reliable video encoding and decoding and external displays, charging to 100% at normal temperatures, and text in the app center again.

## Changes

- **Camera exposure**: auto exposure no longer flickers around bright areas; after the lens is covered or pointed at a light it returns to normal brightness in about 1–3 seconds.
- **Rear camera brightness**: finer rear camera gain steps for smoother brightness changes.
- **Camera information**: both cameras report the correct frame size and frame rate to applications, and incomplete frames are no longer passed on.
- **Video**: hardware decoding correctly finishes the old picture sequence when the video resolution changes; hardware encoding can produce a key frame on request, so streams and screen recordings recover faster.
- **External displays**: corrects error handling and resource cleanup when the USB-C display link fails to start.
- **Battery**: charging reaches 100% at normal temperatures; the charge termination voltage is lowered automatically when the battery reaches 48 °C.
- **App center**: fixes missing text in the app center and other apps that use Flutter's new renderer.
- **Wi-Fi**: fixes repeated timeouts of wireless statistics after the router changes its channel or bandwidth.

## Known limitations

- After a USB-C display has been connected, the tablet must be restarted before a USB cable to a computer is recognized for the developer connection.
- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint and disk hibernation remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap.
